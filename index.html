Hi! Welcome to my page

My name is Renato Ribeiro. I am a Research Engineer at Miraikan - The National Museum of Emerging Science and Innovation in Tokyo, Japan. I received my MSc in Computer Science in 2023, from Faculdade de Ciências da Universidade de Lisboa (FCUL). During my MSc, I was a member of LASIGE at FCUL in the research lab - Tech&People advised by João Guerreiro and André Rodrigues. My research interests lie in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), with a focus on designing, developing, and evaluating accessible virtual environments for blind people. Through collaborations, my contributions also extend to assistive navigation, Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) and tangible and tactile interfaces.
